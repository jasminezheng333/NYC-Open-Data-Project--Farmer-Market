NYC FARMERS MARKETS

NYC Open Data Project

Welcome to this interactive exploration of New York City farmers markets.

This website was created by using public information from NYC open data to provide locations and informations of farmers markets throughout the five boroughs of New York City. Farmers markets play an important role in local communities with fresh fruits, vegetables, baked goods, and other locally produced products while supporting farmers and small businesses.

Using the information presented throughout this website, people can explore where farmers markets are located, which borough have the greatest number of markets, and see how markets are distributed across different neighborhoods. Interactive charts, maps, and visualizations help transform raw data into meaningful insights, making it easier to recognize things that that might not be obvious at first. Whether you are interested in urban planning, public data, food accessibility, or just learning more about this city, the information presented here shows valuable perspectives on the role farmers markets play in New York City.

This project also demonstrates the value of open data as a tool for learning and discovery. Open datasets allow researchers, students, developers, and community members to explore real-world issues and create meaningful applications that inform the public. By transforming a public dataset into an interactive website, this project showcases how data can be used to answer questions, uncover trends, and communicate information in a clear and visually appealing way. The combination of data analysis and visualization helps turn large amounts of information into practical knowledge that users can explore at their own pace.
