NYC FARMERS MARKETS

NYC Farmers Markets Locations and Info


This website uses NYC Open Data to explore farmers markets across NYC. You can use this website to find markets by name, borough, street address, and community district.


This project is about NYC farmers markets and uses open data to show where they are located across the city. It lets users search for markets by name, borough, street address, or community district so the information is easier to find and understand. The goal is to take a large dataset and turn it into a simple website that helps people quickly explore farmers markets in New York City.
