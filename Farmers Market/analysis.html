Description

This website was created by using public information from NYC open data to provide locations and informations of farmers markets throughout the five boroughs of New York City. Using the information presented throughout this website, people can explore where farmers markets are located, which borough have the greatest number of markets, and see how markets are distributed across different neighborhoods.
